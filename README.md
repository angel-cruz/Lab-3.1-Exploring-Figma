## Design Reflection

### 1. What aspects of the design process were most challenging?

The most challenging part was keeping the spacing, alignment, and styles consistent across all three pages.

### 2. How did creating components and adding interactions enhance the usability of your design?

Creating reusable components saved time and helped the pages look consistent. Adding interactions also made it easier for users to understand how to navigate the website.

### 3. How might you use this design as a starting point for future projects?

I can use this design as a basic template for future Bootstrap projects. I can update the colors, content, and components depending on the type of website I am building.
